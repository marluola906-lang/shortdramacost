How to choose in three questions

  1. How many series will you finish in a month? Zero to one → coin purchases for that specific series. Two or more → a subscription, and look hard at the annual tier.
  2. Does English-language audio matter to you? If yes, the originals-first apps are the only real options, and the price gap stops being the deciding factor.
  3. What screen? If it is a television, ShortMax is the only app here that ships a TV build — and we have not tested it. For every other app, vertical video on a horizontal screen is a format mismatch no setting fixes.

Frequently asked questions

Which short-drama app is cheapest?

It depends whether you are committing to a year or finishing one series. The cheapest annual pass we verified is NetShort at $99.99/year, about $0.27 a day. The cheapest way to finish a single series is DramaBox, where one week of membership ($9.99 for the first three weeks, then $12.99) covers as many series as you can watch, against about $30 in one coin pack to finish one series on ReelShort. All figures verified in-app on Android in October 2026.

Which short-drama app has the most free content?

There is no honest single answer, because the number is not fixed. The free window changes with the series: ReelShort let us watch to episode 17 of an 81-episode drama but only to about episode 10 of a 54-episode one, and no app publishes its boundary. Where the paywall landed for us: ReelShort 10–16, DramaBox 11, StardustTV 10, NetShort 9, GoodShort 7, ShortMax 6. No app we tested let us finish a series for free — the free block is a sampler, and the boundary moves title by title.

Do I need a subscription to watch short dramas?

No. Every major app gives free opening episodes on each series, plus daily check-in coins and a limited number of ad unlocks — from five episodes a day on GoodShort and StardustTV to ten on ReelShort and ShortMax. A subscription or coin purchase only becomes necessary when you want to finish a specific series.

Which short-drama app is best for watching on a TV?

One app in this comparison, ShortMax, ships television apps and is therefore the only credible option if a TV is your main screen. We tested the Android phone build only and have not verified how the TV version behaves. For every other app here, vertical video on a horizontal screen is a format mismatch rather than a settings problem.

Are short-drama apps a scam?

The major apps are legitimate businesses with real catalogues. The complaints attached to them are about subscription renewal and cancellation friction, which is a pricing-model issue. The genuine scams in this space are third-party "free coin" and "modded app" sites, not the apps themselves.
