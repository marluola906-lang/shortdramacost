ShortMax Cost Per Season: What One Full Series Really Costs

By marluola Published 28 October 2026 Updated 28 October 2026 Prices checked in-app on 2026-10-09
Short answer
  • There is no separate season to buy — one ShortMax series is the season. The 30-episode title we tested gave 6 free episodes, then 50 coins each.
  • Paying with coins: 24 paid episodes times 50 coins equals 1,200 coins, about $15 (one $9.99 pack plus one $4.99 pack).
  • Free route: 10 ad unlocks a day — the joint-highest allowance we measured, tied with ReelShort — plus a 100-coin welcome bonus. A patient viewer can finish most of a series without paying.
  • VIP only beats coins if you finish the season in under about two weeks. Slower than that, coins are cheaper.
  • ShortMax also has the shortest free opening we measured (6 episodes), so the ad route matters more here than on most rivals.

What we ran into while testing

  • The same store screen printed two per-day figures for the weekly pass at once — $2.86 a day and $2.28 a day — with no explanation of the difference.
  • The intro offer showed $12.79 a week for the first two weeks behind a countdown, then reverted to $19.99.
  • The free opening was the shortest we measured: 6 episodes on a 30-episode series, against 7 on GoodShort, 9 on NetShort and 10 on StardustTV.
  • The ad unlocks opened full-screen third-party install ads. Two of our screenshot attempts were swallowed by a Temu install prompt mid-flow.

“Cost per season” is the question people actually have about ShortMax, and it is the one nobody answers. The app does not publish a monthly price, does not publish a per-series price, and runs its intro offer on a countdown. So we measured it: watched the paywall, counted the episodes, and did the arithmetic on a real series.

What “a season” means here

First, a correction that saves you money. Short-drama apps do not sell seasons the way a television show does. One series is the season. The title we opened ran 30 episodes — but there is no “Season 3” waiting to be sold separately.

That matters because the phrase “cost per season” returns almost nothing accurate. The number to plan around is cost per finished series, not cost per month.

Every figure below comes from an Android emulator running the real Google Play billing SDK with a US account, checked in-app on 9 October 2026, with screenshots at each step. The prices rotate — we saw an intro week at $12.79 and a list price at $19.99 — so treat them as dated measurements, not a rate card.

The per-season formula

Three steps, and you can run them for any series in under a minute:

  1. Find the episode count. It is on the series page. Count the episodes, not the parts.
  2. Subtract the free block. On our series that was 6 episodes before the paywall. The exact number varies by series and is not disclosed in advance, so open the series and scroll to the first locked episode.
  3. Multiply the remainder by 50 coins — the verified per-episode price — then convert to dollars at the pack rate you would actually buy at.

A worked example

This uses the series we actually opened, so the episode count is real rather than illustrative.

A 30-episode series: about $15

The title we measured had 30 episodes and the paywall arrived at episode 7, so 24 episodes needed unlocking. At 50 coins each that is 1,200 coins. Coins are sold in fixed packs, so the total depends on how you assemble it:

Pack combinationCostCoinsCovers the season?
$9.99 + $4.99$14.981,600Yes, with 400 left over
$4.99 x 3$14.971,500Yes, with 300 left over
$19.99 x 1$19.992,400Yes, with 1,200 left over

The single $19.99 pack is the worst per season and the best per coin. That is the trap in this pricing: the pack that offers the best rate is only the best value if you finish another season.

The cheapest route, in order

If the goal is to pay as little as possible per season, do these in order and stop whenever the series is finished:

  1. Watch the free block. 6 episodes at no cost. No action needed.
  2. Use the 10 daily advert-unlocks. This is the most under-used free allowance in the app. Ten episodes a day, free, and a 30-episode series is finishable in a few days without spending anything. The honest cost is time and the advert quality, which on our account was a full-screen Temu install ad.
  3. Spend the welcome bonus. 100 coins on a new account covers two more episodes.
  4. Then buy coins — and buy them from the unlock popup, not the store page. The store page starts at $19.99. The $4.99 and $9.99 packs exist only inside the popup, so buying from the store costs you the worst available rate.
What that means in practice

A viewer who runs the free loop for a week collects up to 70 advert-unlocked episodes plus a 100-coin bonus. Most series we measured do not have that many locked episodes. The paid path exists for impatience, not for access.

When VIP wins per season

VIP is a time-based pass, so its cost per season depends entirely on how fast you watch. Using the rates we recorded:

Compare that to the coin route, which is about $15 per season and does not care how long you take. The rule that falls out of the two tables:

  • Finish a season in under about two weeks → VIP is cheaper.
  • Take a month or more → coins are cheaper, because they do not charge you for the weeks you do not open the app.
  • Cannot say how fast you watch → coins, because they have a ceiling and VIP does not.

Where this breaks down

This is a limitation of the method, not a hedge:

  • The free block is not published, so you cannot compute the cost before you start watching. Every calculation here needs the first locked episode number, and the only way to get it is to open the series. This does not work as a spreadsheet exercise; it is a two-minute check inside the app.
  • These are Android prices from a US-route account. We have not measured iOS billing, and regional pricing is plausible given how other apps in this category behave.
  • The intro price rotates, so any figure you read online is a snapshot. Anchor everything to the coin pack rates and the $19.99 list price.
  • “Cost per season” is not a comparison between apps. Apps with a cheaper season may have a smaller catalogue or shorter episodes. Our free-episode comparison covers the free route across apps.

Bottom line

One full ShortMax series costs about $15 in coins, and nothing on the coin route changes that number based on how long you take. The free allowances — 6 free episodes, 10 advert-unlocks a day, a 100-coin welcome bonus — are large enough that a patient viewer can finish a short series for nothing.

If you want the number for your specific series, you can have it in two minutes: open the series, find the first locked episode, subtract it from the total, multiply by 50 coins, and pick the smallest pack combination that covers it. That is the whole method, and it is more accurate than anything this page — or any price comparison site — can tell you about your own screen.

If your habit turns out to be more than one season a month, the arithmetic changes in a way worth checking before you commit to a weekly tier: see our ShortMax price breakdown for the full picture, and GoodShort vs ShortMax if you are choosing between the two same-priced apps.

Frequently asked questions

How much does one full season of a ShortMax series cost?

Between about $15 and $20 in coins. The 30-episode series we measured had 6 free episodes, leaving 24 paid at 50 coins each, or 1,200 coins. The cheapest pack combination to cover that is one $9.99 pack (1,100 coins) plus one $4.99 pack (500 coins), about $15. One episode costs 50 coins, verified in-app on Android in October 2026.

Why is ShortMax priced per series rather than per month?

Because there is no monthly price. The app sells coins for per-episode unlocks and passes on weekly and annual terms. The useful unit of cost is cost per finished series, which is what this page calculates.

Is it cheaper to buy VIP than coins for one season?

Only if you finish fast. Weekly Pass Pro is $19.99/week and Annual Pass Pro is $149.99/year (about $2.86/week). A 30-episode season watched over two weeks costs about $40 on VIP, more than the $15 coin route. Watched over a few days, VIP can come out ahead. The coin route has a ceiling; VIP does not.

Can I watch a ShortMax series for free?

Partly. The series we tested gave 6 free episodes, then 10 ad unlocks a day — the most generous daily allowance we measured — plus a 100-coin welcome bonus. A patient viewer who watches one or two ads a day can get through a short series without paying, though the ads are full-screen third-party install promotions.

Does ShortMax charge the same price everywhere?

No. We saw a weekly list price of $19.99, an intro popup at $12.79 for the first two weeks, and two different per-day figures printed on the same screen ($2.86 and $2.28 a day). Treat any price you read online, including ours, as a dated measurement rather than a fixed rate.
