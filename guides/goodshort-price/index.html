GoodShort Price: $19.99 a Week, and a Free Route That Could Finish a Series

By marluola Published 26 October 2026 Updated 26 October 2026 Prices checked in-app on 2026-10-09
Short answer
  • GoodShort sells two tiers: Weekly VIP at $19.99 a week and Yearly VIP at $149.99, about $0.41 a day. The yearly pass is exactly seven and a half weeks of the weekly price — so it only wins if you stay past that.
  • But the app will not tell you what you pay after a promo. The popup reads "US$ 19.99/w for 3 weeks, then /w" — and the renewal rate after "then" is blank. The tier table separately says $19.99 a week with auto-renew. The app gives you two answers and no way to reconcile them.
  • The free route adds up to more than a whole series: 7 free episodes, 5 ad unlocks a day for seven days, and 320 check-in coins come to 48.4 episodes against the 43 a 50-episode title needs. And one of our ad unlocks failed with "Ad unlock failed. Please try again later".
  • Coins are 50 per episode — $0.50 from the $4.99 pack, $0.37 from the $29.99 one. A $19.99 pack holds 2,200 coins, which covers the 43 paid episodes of that series with 50 coins to spare.
  • GoodShort is identical to ShortMax on price ($19.99 a week, $149.99 a year) and $7 a week dearer than DramaBox at $12.99. It is also the stingiest with ads: five unlocks a day, against ten for ReelShort and ShortMax.

What we ran into while testing

  • The promo popup prints the price for three weeks and then stops mid-sentence: "US$ 19.99/w for 3 weeks, then /w". The renewal rate is not on the screen anywhere in the app.
  • The tier table and the popup disagree. The table says $19.99 a week with auto-renew; the popup says $19.99 for three weeks and then something else that it declines to name.
  • An ad unlock failed on us — "Ad unlock failed. Please try again later" — with the daily counter already spent. On an app whose free route is this tight, that screen matters.
  • The Top Up screen prints "1 Coin = 1 Bonus" and every pack states its own bonus percentage (+5%, +10%, +35%). We have not seen another app in this category be this explicit about how the maths works.
  • Everything above was captured without logging in. You can read GoodShort pricing, the paywall boundary and the coin packs with no account at all.

The two tiers

GoodShort sells two subscriptions and nothing else — no monthly plan, no mid-tier, no family option:

TierPriceWhat it works out to
Weekly VIP$19.99 a weekabout $2.86 a day, auto-renewing
Yearly VIP$149.99 a yearabout $0.41 a day, auto-renewing

Every price on this page was read inside the app on an Android emulator running the real Google Play billing SDK, on 9 October 2026, in US dollars. We also checked the App Store listing separately: GoodShort is the highest-rated app in this comparison at 4.90 from 529K ratings.

One number does most of the work here: $149.99 is exactly seven and a half weeks of $19.99 (7.50 weeks, 53 days). So the annual pass is not a discount in the usual sense — it is a commitment. If you would have let the weekly subscription run for more than seven and a half weeks, the annual pass is cheaper. If you would have cancelled before that, it is not.

The renewal rate the app leaves blank

This is the thing worth knowing before you tap anything.

GoodShort runs a promo popup with its own countdown. We captured the wording exactly as the app printed it:

“US$ 19.99/w for 3 weeks, then /w”

The renewal rate after “then” is not on the screen. Not in small print, not behind a tap, not anywhere. The sentence simply stops.

And the app gives you a second, different answer one screen away. The tier table says Weekly VIP is $19.99 a week, auto renew. So:

  • The tier table says you will be charged $19.99 every week, forever, until you cancel.
  • The promo popup says you will pay $19.99 for three weeks and then something else — an amount it will not name.
We cannot tell you what the second price is, and neither can the app

The struck-through price behind the “51% OFF” badge was not in frame when we captured the popup, so the base that 51% applies to is also unknown. We are not going to reconstruct it. What we can tell you is that the app itself does not show you the price you are renewing at, and the two screens contradict each other. If that matters to you, screenshot the popup before you accept it, and check the Google Play subscription sheet afterwards — it states the renewal amount in writing.

The free route adds up to a whole series

Here is the part that surprised us most, and it is arithmetic rather than opinion.

GoodShort’s free route has three parts, and on the series we tested they add up to more than the whole show:

SourceWhat you getEpisodes that covers
Free opening episodes7 before the paywall7.0
Ad unlocks, 5 a day for 7 days3535.0
Seven-day check-in sequence320 coins, at 50 coins an episode6.4
Total48.4 episodes

The series we opened was 50 episodes long, with the paywall at episode 8. That leaves 43 episodes to unlock — and the free route covers 48.4.

On paper, GoodShort’s free route can finish a 50-episode series without paying anything.

That is worth saying plainly because it is the opposite of the usual framing. GoodShort has the shortest free opening of the six apps we measured (seven episodes against ReelShort’s ten to sixteen) and the stingiest ad allowance (five a day against ten). It looks like the stingiest app in the category, and on any single measure it is. But the check-in sequence pays more than the ad allowance saves it.

The ad unlock that failed

That margin is thinner than it looks.

One of our ad unlocks failed. The screen read:

“Ad unlock failed. Please try again later”

The daily counter had already been spent. In other words, an unlock can fail after the allowance is used, and the episode stays locked.

Why this matters more on GoodShort than anywhere else

The usual advice about free routes is that you need the ad unlocks and the daily coins together to get through a series. On GoodShort that advice is literally true — 48.4 episodes of allowance against 43 episodes of need is a margin of about five episodes, or roughly one day of ad unlocks. One failed unlock, on one bad afternoon, and the margin is gone. We saw it fail once; we are not going to claim it fails often, because we did not test it often enough to say. We are saying the free route here has no slack in it.

Coins: 50 an episode, and the pack that fits exactly

If you would rather pay per episode than subscribe, one locked episode costs 50 coins or bonus coins — the paywall says so directly. The four packs, with the arithmetic done:

One number here is unusually tidy. The 43 paid episodes of that series need 2,150 coins. The $19.99 pack holds 2,200 — so it covers the whole series and leaves you 50 coins over, which is exactly one more episode. Buy that one pack and you are 50 coins from the next story.

GoodShort is the only app that tells you the bonus percentage

The Top Up screen prints “1 Coin = 1 Bonus” and labels every pack with its own bonus: +5%, +10%, +35%. Nobody else in this category shows you the arithmetic that way. The consequence is that you can compare GoodShort’s packs against every other app’s on the same basis, and GoodShort’s best per-episode price comes out at $0.37 — level with ShortMax, behind DramaBox’s $0.36, and ahead of ReelShort’s $0.40.

320 coins a week is 6.4 episodes. And it is worth noticing where the rest of the free currency comes from: the Earn Rewards page pays out through a third-party offerwall (adje, tapjoy) — 1,000+ bonus coins for playing their games, 5 for reading a novel, 4 for watching an ad.

That is a different kind of 'free' than we mean elsewhere on this site

Offerwall coins are the most generous part of GoodShort’s free economy and the least like money. You are being paid to install and play other people’s games and to hand over your attention. We count them because they are real and they are in the app, but if you are installing an offerwall to earn short-drama coins, you should know what the trade actually is.

Two discount numbers that disagree

GoodShort shows two different discount percentages in two different places, and neither is more authoritative than the other:

  • The home screen popup reads “Today’s Special Offer 50% OFF”
  • The in-drama popup reads “51% OFF”

Neither of them shows the struck-through price, so neither percentage can be checked. The 51% badge is also the one sitting next to a renewal rate that does not exist, which makes it the more confusing of the two.

One thing GoodShort does do better than most

The onboarding flow asks you to pick your preferences first — a wall of tags like “More fantasy” and “More Romance” before you see a single drama. It is the most carefully built first-run flow of the four apps we tested, and it is the reason GoodShort feels like a different kind of product from the same category. The interface is genuinely the most polished we saw, which is why it scores 8.2 in our ranking despite the stingiest ad allowance.

What this page does not cover

  • These are Android, US-account prices from 9 October 2026. We saw GoodShort running two different discount percentages on the same day and a promo popup whose renewal rate was not displayed. Re-check in the app before you buy.
  • We did not test iOS pricing. Apple’s commission sometimes produces different in-app prices, and we will not claim a number we have not seen.
  • The free-route arithmetic is one series. The 48.4-episode figure comes from a 50-episode title with the paywall at episode 8. A longer series with the same opening needs more, and an app that shifts the boundary would change the answer. The numbers are a worked example, not a rule.
  • We saw one ad unlock fail, and we did not test how often that happens. We are reporting what we saw, not a failure rate. If that margin matters to your plan, treat it as thinner than the arithmetic suggests.
  • The 51% badge has no visible base price, so the discount cannot be reconstructed. We have not estimated it.
  • When this breakdown does not work for you: if you are outside the US, the currency and the promo may both be different, and none of the figures here apply. And if you are going to subscribe and forget to cancel, the price is not the thing to focus on — read how to stop a subscription before it renews first.
  • This page does not tell you whether GoodShort is worth watching — only what it costs. For that, see our full GoodShort review.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GoodShort cost?

In our in-app test on Android (9 October 2026), Weekly VIP was $19.99 a week and Yearly VIP was $149.99 a year, about $0.41 a day. The yearly pass works out to exactly seven and a half weeks of the weekly price, so it is the cheaper option only if you would have kept the subscription running that long.

What does GoodShort cost per episode?

50 coins, and the paywall states it as "50 Coins or Bonus". Against the $4.99 pack of 500 coins that is about $0.50 an episode; against the $29.99 pack of 4,050 coins it is about $0.37. Note that GoodShort prices its bonus coins more transparently than most of the category — each pack prints its bonus percentage.

Can you watch a whole series on GoodShort for free?

On the series we tested, the free route adds up to enough. Seven episodes were free before the paywall, five ad unlocks a day for a week is 35 more, and the seven-day check-in sequence pays 320 coins, which is 6.4 episodes at 50 coins each — 48.4 episodes in total, against the 43 a 50-episode title needs. The catch is that our ad unlocks failed once, and the free allowance can be wasted when that happens.

Is GoodShort cheaper than DramaBox?

No. DramaBox costs $12.99 a week on renewal against GoodShort $19.99, and its annual pass is $119.99 against GoodShort $149.99. GoodShort is exactly the same price as ShortMax, and it is the most polished interface we tested, with the highest App Store rating of the six at 4.90 from 529K ratings.

What happens if I cancel GoodShort?

The subscription is billed through Google Play and the cancel button lives in the store, not in the app. GoodShort allows five ad unlocks a day, half what ReelShort and ShortMax allow, and the weekly tier auto-renews. If you are going to subscribe and forget, read our guide to stopping a subscription before it renews before you tap join.
