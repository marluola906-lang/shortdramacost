ReelShort Coins vs VIP: Which One Is Cheaper for How Many Episodes

By marluola Published 13 October 2026 Updated 16 October 2026 Prices checked in-app on 2026-10-13
Short answer
  • The break-even point is roughly five episodes. Under that, coins are cheaper. Over it, VIP wins — and the free daily coins cover part of the coin route for free.
  • One ReelShort episode costs 60 coins, and the smallest coin pack is $4.99 for 500, so a single episode is about $0.60. The large $49.99 pack drops that to $0.40.
  • VIP renewal started at $5.99–$19.99 per week depending on the tier we saw. That only beats coins if you will unlock more than about 8–10 episodes in that week.
  • Coins are usually the better first choice because they do not auto-renew. VIP charges you again whether or not you open the app.
  • The free route is more generous than most people use: 50 coins on first login, up to 200 on day 7 of daily check-ins, and 10 ad-unlocks a day.

What we ran into while testing

  • A $4.99 pack of 500 coins works out at about $0.0100 a coin. The $49.99 pack of 7,500 works out at $0.0067 — the same coins, half the unit price.
  • One coin pack promotion was 1,000 coins plus 3,000 back over seven sign-in days. Miss a day and the bonus does not arrive.
  • On ReelShort the $4.99 and $9.99 coin packs only appear inside the locked-episode popup. The Store front page does not show them.
  • ReelShort scores 4.7 on the App Store and 3.8 on Google Play. The same app scores 1.3 on Trustpilot, where 265 reviews complain almost entirely about billing and cancellation.

Three ways to unlock one episode

When an episode locks, ReelShort offers three buttons, and they cost very different amounts:

  • 60 coins — priced directly on the paywall
  • Watch an advert — limited to 10 per day
  • Go VIP — unlocks everything, auto-renewing

Most people try the first two, then subscribe without checking the arithmetic. Here is the arithmetic.

Everything below comes from one Android account in October 2026, US Google Play billing, with screenshots at each step. Prices rotate, so treat these as dated measurements.

Two things this table exposes that the store screen does not:

The small pack is a trap

The $4.99 pack costs 50% more per coin than the $49.99 pack — $0.0100 against $0.0067. Yet the small pack is what you see first when you open a locked episode. You pay 49% more per episode by buying the pack that is easiest to find.

And the small packs are hidden

The store page does not show the $4.99 tier at all. It appears only when you open a locked episode and choose to top up from there. A user who buys from the store page without checking pays the worst rate available.

What VIP actually costs

This is where most comparisons go wrong, because they use the intro price. The weekly VIP rates we recorded after the first discounted week:

At $0.60 per episode on coins, a week of VIP works out as:

  • $5.99 plan breaks even at about 10 episodes a week
  • $11.99 plan at about 20 episodes a week
  • $19.99 plan at about 33 episodes a week

Where the break-even point is

Pulling it together, and using the middle case ($11.99/week against coins at $0.55 each):

  • 1–5 episodes: coins, clearly. The subscription costs more before you have watched anything.
  • 5–8 episodes: close enough that the deciding factor becomes risk, not price — coins do not auto-renew.
  • 8+ episodes in a week: VIP, clearly.
The honest framing

The break-even point is not a fixed number, because the VIP rate you get is whatever intro offer your account lands on, and the same account saw 67% off one day and 21% off the next. What is stable is the structure: coins are cheaper for casual viewing, VIP is cheaper for bingeing.

Which to pick

  1. Watch the free episodes first. Ten to sixteen before the paywall, and the free allowance varies by series, so check the specific show.

  2. Run the daily loop for a week. Check-ins plus watch-time tasks plus ten ad-unlocks produces more coins than most casual viewers use. Do this before deciding anything.

  3. Under about eight episodes a week: buy coins, and buy a large pack. Not the $4.99 tier you will see first — work out which pack size matches your remaining episode count.

  4. Bingeing weekly: VIP is cheaper, but only once you are past roughly eight episodes in that week. If your bingeing is intermittent, coins still win because they do not charge you for the weeks you do not watch.

  5. Never let a subscription run untouched — the renewal lands 24 hours before the period ends at the renewal rate, not the intro rate.

Limits of this comparison

A caveat that applies to every number here: this comparison assumes you watch on Android with US Google Play billing, and if you do not, it does not work for you. It does not hold on iOS, where Apple’s billing and pricing differ, and it does not hold in other regions — two of the apps we tested price by the country your traffic exits from. The further limits:

  • One account, one region, October 2026. The renewal rates we recorded came from that week’s intro offer. We have less data on how stable renewal rates are than on how stable the coin prices are.
  • Watch-time tasks and ad limits are the kind of thing that changes without notice. We confirmed 10 ad-unlocks twice, on two separate days, but that cap is set by the app and can be altered at any time.
  • We did not test whether ad-unlocks can be farmed. The daily cap makes repeated attempts pointless, but we only observed the limit, we did not try to push against it.
  • The advert quality is part of the real cost. On our account the ads served were a fake insurance offer and a TikTok install prompt. If that matters to you, the “free” route is not really free.
  • This is not an endorsement. ReelShort is affiliate-linked on this site, and the recommendation to start with coins and exhaust the free route is the one that costs us the least commission.

When this calculation does not apply

  • If you are on iOS. Apple’s billing is a different system. Our iOS knowledge in this project comes from Apple’s documentation, not from testing.
  • If you are outside the US. These are US-route prices. Your local pricing may be lower, and ReelShort’s prices moved with our exit country in testing.
  • If you only want one series and it has 12 episodes left. Then the maths is trivial: multiply by 60 coins and buy the pack that covers it. No subscription logic needed.
  • If you are reading this months from now. The structure will still hold — intro below renewal, cheaper per coin in bigger packs, free episodes varying by series — but every specific number will have moved.
  • If you care about catalogue size more than cost. VIP unlocks 16,000-plus titles on some apps; a coin balance buys you one series. That is not a cost comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to buy coins or subscribe to VIP on ReelShort?

Below roughly five episodes, coins. Above that, VIP. A single ReelShort episode costs 60 coins, which is about $0.60 at the smallest pack or $0.40 in the largest. VIP renewal ran $5.99 to $19.99 per week depending on the tier, so it only works out cheaper if you unlock more than about 8 to 10 episodes that week. Coins also do not auto-renew, so they are the lower-risk choice for a casual viewer.

How many coins is one episode on ReelShort?

60 coins. The paywall states the price directly, so it is not a hidden number. Be aware that DramaBox and several other apps charge 50 coins per episode while NetShort charges 98, so a coin balance is not portable between apps.

Is it worth buying the big coin pack?

Only if you finish the series. Per-coin cost falls as the pack grows: $4.99 for 500 coins works out at about $0.0100 a coin, while $49.99 for 7,500 works out at about $0.0067, roughly a third cheaper. The small packs sit at the top of the store page precisely because they are the worst value.

Can I watch ReelShort without paying at all?

More often than the apps suggest. We recorded 50 coins for first login, a seven-day check-in sequence reaching 200 coins on day 7, watch-time tasks worth up to 340 coins a day, and 10 ad-unlocks per day. The free episodes before the paywall ranged from 10 to 16 depending on the series. That is often enough for a casual viewer to never pay.

What happens if I subscribe and stop watching?

The renewal charge lands 24 hours before each period ends, at the renewal rate rather than the intro rate, which can be up to three times higher. Coins do not carry that risk because they do not renew. If you are unsure, coins are the safer route.
