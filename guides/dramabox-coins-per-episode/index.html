How Many Coins Per Episode on DramaBox? 50 — and What a Coin Really Costs

By marluola Published 25 October 2026 Updated 25 October 2026 Prices checked in-app on 2026-10-09
Short answer
  • One locked episode on DramaBox costs 50 coins. That number did not change across the titles we opened — what varies is what a coin costs you.
  • 50 coins is a quantity, not a price. Depending on which pack you buy, one episode costs between $0.50 and $0.36 — a 29% swing in the unit price that you control, not the app.
  • The cleanest thing we found in this whole category: every paid coin costs exactly one cent — 21 packs across five apps all divide out to $0.00997–$0.01000. A pack price is not a price per coin; it is a bonus ratio with a price attached.
  • ReelShort charges 60 coins per episode, so the same episode costs $0.50 against $0.60 at the entry pack, and $0.36 against $0.40 at the top of each ladder.
  • For one 43-episode series with an 11-episode free opening, the paid tail is 1,600 coins — $12.99 if you buy the pack that matches it exactly, or $0.36 an episode if the balance carries into the next series.

What we ran into while testing

  • The 50-coin price is not on the coin pack screen. It only appears on the paywall after you have already opened a locked episode — the store page shows packs and nothing that converts them into episodes.
  • The two numbers live in different places in the app. There is no coins-per-episode line anywhere on the pack screen, which is why the per-episode figure has to be worked out by hand.
  • The same paywall carries a daily ad counter of 8 next to the 50-coin price, so the first question on that screen is not really how many coins — it is whether an ad will do it instead.
  • When you unlock, the app spends reward coins before paid coins. The 50 coins come out of your free balance first, and the paid pack only starts biting once that balance is dry.
  • The pack screen showed yen and dollars on the same device one day apart, so every per-episode figure here is only correct for a reader on a US account.

The answer is 50, and that is the easy part

One locked episode on DramaBox costs 50 coins. The paywall states it as “Price: 50 Coins”, and it did not change across the titles we opened.

The catch is that 50 coins is not a price — it is a quantity. What an episode costs you depends entirely on which coin pack you buy, and the range is wide:

  • $0.50 an episode from the smallest pack
  • $0.36 an episode from the largest

That is a 29% swing in the unit price, and the app never shows you the per-episode figure anywhere. It shows you packs.

Twenty-one packs, five apps, and every single paid coin costs one cent. NetShort is the one we cannot check — its Top Up page shows the two subscription tiers and no coin packs at all, so there is nothing to divide.

The pack prices are not different prices for the same coin. They are the same price, and what changes is how many coins you buy and how many the pack gives away free.

This is why the per-coin prices people quote look so different from each other. A pack’s headline unit price is not a price at all — it is one cent divided by the bonus ratio. Which means the only question worth asking is: how many bonus coins does this pack throw in?

What a whole series costs

The question that actually matters: what does a complete series cost in coins?

The first DramaBox series we opened was 43 episodes with the paywall at episode 12. The paid tail is therefore 32 episodes, which is 32 × 50 = 1,600 coins.

Two conclusions that the app never spells out.

First, the $12.99 pack is the cheapest way to buy exactly one series — 1,600 coins, no leftover, $12.99. The entry pack looks cheaper per episode but you need six of them and $17.94 comes out the other end.

Second, the $24.99 pack is the cheapest per episode, but only if you will actually use it — 3,500 coins is 70 episodes. If you watch one series and stop, you have paid $12 more for the privilege of a lower unit price you never use.

So the naive advice to buy the biggest pack is wrong for a one-series buyer and right for a binge. Buy the pack that matches the tail you actually have, not the biggest one on the screen.

The free route, and why reward coins come first

50 coins is only 50 coins if you have none — and you start with none.

The first week of free coins on DramaBox adds up to 825 coins: 450 from the five one-time sign-up tasks, 165 from seven days of check-ins, and 210 from the post-check-in ad each day. At 50 coins an episode, 825 coins is 16.5 episodes.

That is a real fortnight of viewing, and it is the reason the coin route is worth understanding before you spend anything. The daily ladder and the task list are in DramaBox free coins.

And the ad route bypasses coins entirely. DramaBox lets you unlock 8 episodes a day with an ad — ReelShort allows 10. The paywall offers all three options side by side: 50 coins, watch an ad, or join.

One mechanic that changes your effective price: when you unlock, the app spends reward coins before paid coins. The 50 coins for your next episode come out of your free balance first, so a good week of check-ins can carry you two full series without touching a paid coin. Your paid pack only starts being spent once that balance is dry.

When coins stop making sense

If you are going to watch more than about eight series a year, coins stop making sense.
  • One series, paid tail: $12.99 — or $11.42 of a $24.99 pack if the rest carries forward
  • Annual membership: $119.99, which works out at $2.31 a week
  • Break-even: eight to eleven series a year

There is a second reason membership beats coins even below that threshold: as a member you never meet a paywall, so the 8 free ad unlocks a day are not something you have to spend an episode on either.

What this page does not cover

  • These are Android, US-account prices from 9 October 2026. Coin pack prices and bonus ratios are exactly the kind of thing an app adjusts for a campaign. We bought all four packs on 9 October and the ladder has held since, but re-check in the app before you buy.
  • 50 coins per episode held across every title we opened. Some promotional or members-only series may price differently, and series length varies widely, so per-series figures are worked examples rather than a guarantee.
  • We did not test iOS pricing. Apple’s commission sometimes produces different in-app prices, and we will not claim a number we have not seen.
  • When this breakdown does not work for you: if you are a member, the 50 coins never applies — membership removes the per-episode charge entirely. And if your screen shows a different currency, the dollar figures here are simply not your figures.
  • We did not test what happens to an unspent coin balance — for instance after you cancel a subscription, or if a series is pulled. We did not want to guess, so treat any leftover balance as at risk.

Frequently asked questions

How many coins does one episode cost on DramaBox?

50 coins. The paywall shows it as "Price: 50 Coins" and it held across every title we opened on Android in October 2026. What that costs in dollars depends entirely on which coin pack you buy: about $0.50 an episode from the $2.99 pack and about $0.36 from the $24.99 pack.

Why do DramaBox coin packs have different prices per coin?

They do not. Every paid coin costs one cent in every pack — $2.99 for 300 coins, $6.99 for 700, $12.99 for 1,300 and $24.99 for 2,500. The apparent difference in unit price comes entirely from the bonus coins thrown in with the larger packs, which is why the $24.99 pack works out to $0.0071 a coin and the $2.99 pack to $0.0100.

Is it cheaper to buy a bigger coin pack?

Per episode, yes. The $24.99 pack is $0.36 an episode against $0.50 for the $2.99 pack. But if you are only going to watch one series, the $12.99 pack holds exactly 1,600 coins — one full paid tail — and costs less than buying six small packs at $17.94.

How many DramaBox coins do I get for free?

The first week adds up to 825 coins if you clear every route: 450 from the one-time sign-up tasks, 165 from seven days of check-ins and 210 from the post-check-in ad each day. At 50 coins an episode that is 16.5 episodes. On top of that, eight episodes a day can be unlocked with an ad instead of coins.

How many DramaBox coins for a whole series?

Our first series was 43 episodes with the paywall at episode 12, so the paid tail is 32 episodes, or 1,600 coins. That is one $12.99 pack exactly, or about $11.42 of a $24.99 pack if the remaining balance carries into the next series.
