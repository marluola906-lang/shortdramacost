GoodShort vs ShortMax: Which One Costs Less to Watch

By marluola Published 28 October 2026 Updated 28 October 2026 Prices checked in-app on 2026-10-09
Short answer
  • Per episode they are identical: both charge 50 coins, so the smallest pack costs $0.50 an episode and the largest about $0.37 on both.
  • Subscriptions are the same too: $19.99 a week and $149.99 a year on both — pricing across this category has converged.
  • The difference is the free route. GoodShort gives 7 free episodes and a 320-coin weekly check-in, but only 5 ad unlocks a day that failed once in our test.
  • ShortMax gives 6 free episodes but 10 ad unlocks a day — the most generous allowance we measured — at the cost of intrusive full-screen install ads.
  • Pick GoodShort if you collect daily and hate ads; pick ShortMax if you will watch ads to avoid paying. On pure price they are a dead heat.

What we ran into while testing

  • Both apps printed $19.99 a week and $149.99 a year on the same day — the category has converged on one price.
  • GoodShort gave 7 free episodes of a 50-episode drama and locked from episode 8; ShortMax gave 6 of a 30-episode drama.
  • GoodShort allows five ad unlocks a day, half of ShortMax, and our ad attempt failed outright with an error message.
  • ShortMax opened full-screen third-party install ads on the ad route — two of our screenshots were swallowed by a Temu install prompt mid-flow.
How we measured this

Every figure on this page comes from one hands-on session per app, not from a review site. The setup was identical throughout:

  • Environment — Android in BlueStacks, real Google Play billing, signed into a US Google Play account so prices rendered in dollars. No VPN region-switching during the pricing pass.
  • Deriving the per-episode price — every app prices locked episodes in coins, not dollars. We read the coin cost per episode from the unlock screen, then divided by the coins-per-dollar of a given pack. That is why the same episode shows two prices on our tables: $0.50 is the entry pack, and the lower figure is what the largest pack works out to. Both are real, and which one applies depends on the pack you buy.
  • Renewal, not intro — where an app offers a discounted first period, we report the renewal price separately. Intro offers in this category carry countdown timers and rotate by day and by account, so they are treated as marketing, not as the price.
  • Confirmed against the store sheet — for every subscription we cross-checked the renewal figure against the Google Play subscription sheet, which is the number that actually reaches the card. Where the in-app text disagreed with the sheet, we report the sheet.

Known limits of this method — three things we could not control, stated so you can weigh the numbers: introductory offers rotate daily; DramaBox prices by IP region rather than by account, so a different connection can quote a different currency; and neither app publishes its free/paid boundary, so that figure varies by series and is a measured sample rather than a rule.

The short answer

These two apps are closer than the logos suggest, and the closeness is the useful finding. Both charge 50 coins per locked episode — identical, episode for episode. If you buy coins, you are buying the same thing on either app.

Where they diverge is the free route. GoodShort gives a larger check-in but a smaller ad allowance; ShortMax gives the opposite. And the subscriptions are literally the same price: $19.99 a week and $149.99 a year on both.

One honest caveat before the tables

Both apps’ promotional popups leave the renewal price ambiguous. GoodShort’s reads “US$ 19.99/w for 3 weeks, then /w” with the renewal figure missing. ShortMax’s intro shows $12.79/week for two weeks behind a countdown. We photograph both and treat $19.99 as the ongoing weekly figure on each — the worst-case assumption.

The real difference is not the coins

If you are deciding between these two apps on the strength of a per-episode table, the table is telling you they are the same. They are. Pick on the subscription price (identical) and the free tier (different) instead.

GoodShort's ad unlock failed in our test

We captured an “Ad unlock failed. Please try again later” message on GoodShort. When that happens the episode does not unlock, and depending on the failure it can consume part of the daily allowance. It is a good reason not to rely on GoodShort’s ad route as your only way to finish a series.

Which one to pick

In order of how likely each factor is to decide it for you:

  1. You will watch ads to avoid paying — pick ShortMax. Ten unlocks a day against five, and a stable route against GoodShort’s failure.

  2. You open the app daily and collect — consider GoodShort. Its 320-coin check-in week is the one genuine advantage it holds, worth more than the shorter free block costs.

  3. You will subscribe — they are the same app. $19.99 a week and $149.99 a year on both. Pick on which free route you will actually use.

  4. You dislike intrusive ads — lean GoodShort. ShortMax’s free route is full-screen third-party install promotions, which we lost two screenshots to.

What this comparison cannot tell you

  • GoodShort’s renewal price is unconfirmed. Everything here uses the promotional $19.99 as the ongoing figure. If the real renewal is lower, the gap narrows — but it cannot change the fact that both annual passes are $149.99.

  • One series per app. Our free-episode counts come from a 50-episode title on GoodShort and a 30-episode title on ShortMax. Neither app publishes its free block and both move by title, so treat these as samples.

  • Android only, October 2026. Both were tested through Google Play on an emulator with real billing. We have not verified iOS pricing.

  • Content quality is subjective and mostly untested. We are a price comparison. Where the apps differ on production values or originals, we have not done the testing to tell you which is better.

Bottom line

Per episode, they are the same app. Fifty coins each, $0.50 on the smallest pack, and within four cents of each other at the best rate. Nothing here justifies switching for the coins.

Per subscription, they are the same app too. $149.99 a year on both, $19.99 a week on both. The only price difference is ShortMax’s short-term intro week.

The free route is the entire decision. ShortMax wins on ad unlocks (10 a day, stable); GoodShort wins on check-in (320 coins a week) but loses on ad reliability (5 a day, failed once) and ad quality. So: watching ads to avoid paying, take ShortMax. Collecting daily streaks and nothing else, GoodShort is defensible. And check the renewal price in-app before you commit to either — we could not confirm GoodShort’s, and neither can you from the app’s own text.

Frequently asked questions

Is ShortMax cheaper than GoodShort?

No, not on anything either app publishes. Both charge 50 coins per episode, both list a weekly pass at $19.99 and an annual pass at $149.99. The only place they differ is the free route, and there ShortMax gives more ad unlocks (10 a day against 5) while GoodShort gives a larger check-in (320 coins a week against ShortMax 100 on sign-up). On price they are the same app with different free tiers.

How much is one episode on GoodShort vs ShortMax?

Exactly the same: 50 coins on both. On the smallest pack both are $0.50 per episode. On the largest pack GoodShort is $29.99 for 4,050 coins (about $0.37 an episode) and ShortMax is $24.99 for 3,375 coins (also about $0.37). Because the coin price is identical, the episode price is identical — the difference is in the subscriptions and the free tier.

Which has more free episodes?

Close, and both are thin. GoodShort gave 7 free episodes of a 50-episode series; ShortMax gave 6 of a 30-episode series. Neither publishes its free block and both vary by title, so treat these as measured samples rather than fixed rules. The bigger gap is ad unlocks: ShortMax allows 10 a day, GoodShort 5.

Is GoodShort worth it over ShortMax?

Only for one specific reason: its check-in pays 320 coins across seven days against ShortMax 100 on sign-up. If you open the app daily and collect them, that gap is real money. In exchange you pay the same subscription and get half the ad unlocks. For most people finishing one or two series, the two are interchangeable on price.

Does ShortMax work on a television?

The publisher advertises television apps, which is unusual in this category, but we tested only the Android phone build and cannot confirm how the TV version behaves. Treat TV support as unverified by us.
