DramaBox vs NetShort: Which One Costs Less to Watch

What we ran into while testing

  • NetShort quoted five weekly prices in one session: $7.99, $14.99, $14.39, $15.99 and $24.99. The Play sheet confirmed the renewal at $24.99/week plus tax.
  • NetShort charges 98 N coins per episode — nearly double DramaBox 50 — and the top-up page never listed coin packs, so we could not price the coin route in advance.
  • NetShort gives 9 free episodes of a 57-episode drama; DramaBox gave about 11 of a 43-episode drama, then 50 coins per episode.
  • NetShort unlock ads ran to three minutes — the longest we sat through — and its bonus coins expired after seven days.
How we measured this

Every figure on this page comes from one hands-on session per app, not from a review site. The setup was identical throughout:

  • Environment — Android in BlueStacks, real Google Play billing, signed into a US Google Play account so prices rendered in dollars. No VPN region-switching during the pricing pass.
  • Deriving the per-episode price — every app prices locked episodes in coins, not dollars. We read the coin cost per episode from the unlock screen, then divided by the coins-per-dollar of a given pack. That is why the same episode shows two prices on our tables: $0.50 is the entry pack, and the lower figure is what the largest pack works out to. Both are real, and which one applies depends on the pack you buy.
  • Renewal, not intro — where an app offers a discounted first period, we report the renewal price separately. Intro offers in this category carry countdown timers and rotate by day and by account, so they are treated as marketing, not as the price.
  • Confirmed against the store sheet — for every subscription we cross-checked the renewal figure against the Google Play subscription sheet, which is the number that actually reaches the card. Where the in-app text disagreed with the sheet, we report the sheet.

Known limits of this method — three things we could not control, stated so you can weigh the numbers: introductory offers rotate daily; DramaBox prices by IP region rather than by account, so a different connection can quote a different currency; and neither app publishes its free/paid boundary, so that figure varies by series and is a measured sample rather than a rule.

The short answer

These two apps invert the usual pattern. DramaBox is the cheaper one week to week and per episode, but NetShort hides the cheapest annual pass in the entire comparison. So the answer to “which costs less” depends entirely on how long you plan to watch.

The useful finding: DramaBox wins on the weekly pass and the coins; NetShort wins only on the annual commitment. If you know you will stay a year, NetShort’s $99.99 is the best long-term price we found anywhere. If you are not sure, DramaBox is the safer buy.

Read NetShort's Play sheet, not its popups

NetShort quoted us five weekly prices in one session. The only one that reaches your card is the Google Play renewal figure: $24.99/week plus tax. Everything else is an intro or a popup. We lead with the Play sheet number because it is the one you will actually pay.

NetShort's weekly price is a moving target

$24.99/week is what the Google Play sheet said you will renew at. But we also saw $7.99, $14.99, $14.39 and $15.99 in the app the same day. None of those change the annual maths, but they make the weekly tier a thing to read carefully before you agree to it.

Which one to pick

  1. You will watch for a full year — pick NetShort. $99.99 is the cheapest annual pass in the comparison, $20 under DramaBox and $100 under ReelShort.

  2. You watch week to week — pick DramaBox. $12.99 against $24.99 a week, and a per-episode coin price less than half of NetShort’s.

  3. You finish one series — pick DramaBox. $12.99 in coins against NetShort’s unpriced and expensive 98-N route.

  4. You want clarity — pick DramaBox. NetShort shows five weekly prices and hides its coin packs; DramaBox’s pricing is the simplest we measured after GoodShort.

What this comparison cannot tell you

  • NetShort’s coin packs are not published. We could not price the per-episode dollar cost, only the 98 N coin count. Treat any “NetShort costs $X per episode” claim from a third party with suspicion — we measured five different weekly prices and one external review quoting about $30/week that matched none of them.

  • One series per app. Our free-episode counts come from a 43-episode title on DramaBox and a 57-episode title on NetShort. Neither app publishes its free block and both move by title, so treat these as samples.

  • Android only, October 2026. Both were tested through Google Play on an emulator with real billing. We have not verified iOS pricing.

  • Content quality is outside our scope. We are a price comparison. Where the apps differ on catalogue or production values, we have not tested it.

Bottom line

Per episode, DramaBox wins decisively — 50 coins against 98 N, and NetShort will not even show you the coin pack prices.

Per week, DramaBox wins — $12.99 against $24.99, more than the cost of a Netflix subscription each week for NetShort.

Per year, NetShort wins — $99.99 against $119.99, the cheapest annual pass in this comparison. That single number is the reason NetShort belongs on the list at all.

So: commit to a year, take NetShort; anything shorter, take DramaBox. And read NetShort’s Google Play confirmation sheet before you agree to the weekly price — it is the only number in that app you can trust, and we verified it against the in-app quote.

Frequently asked questions

Is NetShort cheaper than DramaBox?

For a full year, yes — NetShort annual is $99.99 against DramaBox $119.99, the cheapest year in this comparison. For anything shorter, no: NetShort renews at $24.99 a week against DramaBox $12.99, and its per-episode coin price is 98 N coins against DramaBox 50 — nearly double. So NetShort wins only if you commit to twelve months; week to week, DramaBox is far cheaper.

How much is one episode on DramaBox vs NetShort?

DramaBox is 50 coins (about $0.50 at the smallest pack). NetShort is 98 N coins — the most expensive per-episode unlock we measured across the six apps, and the N coin has no published pack price, so its dollar cost cannot be worked out in advance. On coins alone, DramaBox is less than half the price per episode.

Why does NetShort show so many different prices?

In one session on one account we were quoted $7.99, $14.99, $14.39, $15.99 and $24.99 a week. They are real offers applying to different stages of the same subscription. The renewal price on the Google Play confirmation sheet — $24.99/week plus tax from 16 October 2026 — is the only one that describes what you keep paying, and it is the figure we lead with.

Which has more free episodes?

Close. NetShort gave 9 free episodes of a 57-episode series; DramaBox gave about 11 of a 43-episode series. Neither publishes its free block and both vary by title, so treat these as measured samples. DramaBox also allows 8 ad unlocks a day against NetShort two ads per episode with no published daily cap.

Is NetShort worth it over DramaBox?

Only if you will watch for a full year — that is the one place NetShort is cheaper ($99.99 against $119.99). For a month or a single series, DramaBox is cheaper on both the weekly pass and the coins. NetShort also does not publish coin pack prices in the top-up flow, so you cannot price the coin route before subscribing.
